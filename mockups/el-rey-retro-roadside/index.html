The menu

Pull up hungry.

From a chorizo breakfast burrito to weekend barbacoa.

Tortas

Huge portions and the famous Torta Cubana. This is El Rey de las Tortas, after all.

Tacos

Al pastor, asada, chicken and tripe. Asada tacos come with grilled onions and chips on the side.

Burritos

Carne asada burritos and breakfast burritos, including chorizo.

Weekend specials

Barbacoa de res, borrego en penca, barbacoa plate and consomé de borrego. Saturday and Sunday.

Drinks & raspados

Licuados y biónicos, jugos naturales and aguas frescas artesanales, including agua de Jamaica.

Salsa bar

Salsas estilo rancho, a salsa bar and free charro beans.

El Reto: two Tortas Cubanas.

The challenge on El Rey's flyer: finish two Tortas Cubanas by yourself and you don't pay. “¡Si te comes 2 tortas cubanas tú solo, no pagas!”
