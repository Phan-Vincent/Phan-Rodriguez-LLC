03 / The routeHeaders to tailpipe

The whole route.
Not just the box.

A muffler is one part of a system. Flanges, pipe diameter, bends, hangers and clearance all affect fit. The shop looks at the run as a whole before recommending work.

i / Form

Look first.

The vehicle goes up. The existing route, clearances, flanges and hangers are inspected before the job is scoped.

ii / Fit

Measure twice.

Pipe and muffler are fitted to the car, not chosen from a one-size list. Bends and hangers have to work in the space that is there.

iii / Weld

Close the joint.

Joints are welded and checked. Review topics on the shop's Google listing include welds and custom exhaust work.
