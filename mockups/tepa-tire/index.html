01 / The shopOntario · Mountain Ave

The kind of shop
that writes it down.

A working tire and auto shop on South Mountain Avenue. Tires are stacked where you can see them; the work is explained before it starts.

Most drivers arrive with one clear problem: a tire that will not hold air, a set that is worn past its tread, or a brake pedal that has started to feel wrong.

Bring the vehicle or the wheel in. Tepa handles new and used tires, patching and flat repair, mounting and balancing, and brake work. If the right answer is a repair rather than a replacement, that conversation happens at the counter — not in a chat window.

Look for the blue-and-white sign and the tire stacks under the red and blue canopies. The shop is open seven days a week, 8 to 6.
