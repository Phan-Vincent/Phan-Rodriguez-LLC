What's it doing?

Pick the closest one. We'll tell you what we check.

Won't start

We test the battery, the starter, the ignition switch and the wiring between them before anything gets replaced.

A real diagnosis needs the car in the shop.

Battery light on

Usually the alternator or its wiring. We measure charging voltage with the engine running.

A real diagnosis needs the car in the shop.

Dead battery every morning

Something is drawing power with the key off. We measure the draw and pull circuits one at a time until it stops.

A real diagnosis needs the car in the shop.

Gauge reads wrong

Fuel, temperature or speed that doesn't match reality. We check the sender, the wiring and the cluster.

A real diagnosis needs the car in the shop.

Windows or locks stuck

Switches, motors and the wiring where the door meets the body, which flexes every time the door opens.

A real diagnosis needs the car in the shop.

Lights out or flickering

Headlights, brake lights, turn signals. We check bulbs, grounds, relays and switches.

A real diagnosis needs the car in the shop.

A/C or blower dead

Blower motor, resistor, relay and controls.

A real diagnosis needs the car in the shop.

Check-engine light

We read the code, then test the sensor and its circuit. A code names a circuit, not a part.

A real diagnosis needs the car in the shop.
