Symptoms

Pick the closest one.

Won't start

We test the battery, the starter, the ignition switch and the wiring between them.

Battery light on

Usually the alternator or its wiring. We measure charging voltage with the engine running.

Dead battery every morning

Something is drawing power with the key off. We measure the draw and pull circuits one at a time.

Gauge reads wrong

We check the sender, the wiring and the cluster.

Windows or locks stuck

Switches, motors and the wiring where the door meets the body.

Lights out or flickering

We check bulbs, grounds, relays and switches.

A/C or blower dead

Blower motor, resistor, relay and controls.

Check-engine light

We read the code, then test the sensor and its circuit.

A real diagnosis needs the car in the shop. No wheel alignments.
