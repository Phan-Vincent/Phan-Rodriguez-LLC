What we fix

  • Bumper repair. Cracks, splits, scrapes and torn mounting tabs, repaired and refinished.
  • Bumper replacement. When a cover is bent or torn past repair, we fit a replacement.
  • Collision repair. Body panels and structure damaged in a hit, straightened and repainted.
  • Dent removal. Door, fender and quarter-panel dents worked back to shape.
  • Chrome delete. Grille, trim and window surrounds refinished in the finish you pick.
  • Auto body restoration. Older paint and bodywork brought back, panel by panel.

Before you call

  1. Have your year, make and model handy.
  2. Tell us which bumper and what happened to it.
  3. A photo helps when you come by.
