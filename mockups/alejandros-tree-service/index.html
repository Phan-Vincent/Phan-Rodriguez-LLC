04 / Before you call

Let’s start
with your tree.

  1. Tell us what you see.

    What kind of tree is it? What would you like to change? A simple description is a good beginning.

  2. Talk about the space.

    Mention nearby buildings, fences and how the team could reach the tree. Share your location to discuss availability.

  3. Agree on the details.

    Confirm the scope, cleanup, price and timing directly with the business before any work begins.
