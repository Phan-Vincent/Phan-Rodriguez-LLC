# Business research — November 9, 2026 (Pacific)

## Scope and method

294 source lead records dated November 8 were reconciled against local mockup folders. The initial repository contained seven concepts for six businesses. The first batch selects three businesses, not every unmatched lead. The lack of a mockup is not proof that a business currently lacks an official website.

Supervisor inspected the three Google Maps profiles in an authenticated Chrome browser, including listing photographs and review topics. No listing photographs are licensed for reuse here. Existing repository desktop/mobile screenshots supplied layout references; no new screenshot attachments were present in this request.

Yelp blocks the web search tool with robots.txt. Direct browser access to Tepa's Yelp page reached a device-verification challenge; it was not bypassed. Yelp content below is from Google's indexed snippets, not a full inspection of the Yelp pages. No exact Yelp review quotes or Yelp ratings should be published on the mockups from this evidence.
