## 7. Design

Chosen direction: **storefront as brand.** Each site's colors, type and logo come from the shop's real sign and building, so the owner recognizes their own shop.

| | Tri Auto | A C Tires | Moreno's |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Palette | Cream `#efe6d3`, ink `#141414`, `#d7261e` `#f26b1d` `#f7b51c` | Blue `#1f3fae`, sign white `#fdfbf4`, red `#d62b1f`, yellow `#ffd23c` | Wall `#f4f1ea`, red `#c81f25`, yellow `#ffd21f`, blue `#1d4fb8` |
| Type | Heavy italic capitals | Condensed block capitals | Block capitals on banner colors |
| Hero | Slanted photo of hands on a wrench, three stripes on its edge, rating badge | Round photo of a tire being fitted, "Open til 8 PM" badge | Wide shop-floor photo with banners unfurling over it |

Shared features: words of the headline rise in on load, sections reveal on scroll, ratings count up, one marquee per site, live "Open now / Closed" status in Pacific time with today's hours highlighted, tap-to-call, Google Maps directions link, skip link and focus rings, all motion off under reduced-motion settings, light film grain.

Patterns taken from other shop sites (Zarla's review of auto repair sites): specialty named in the headline, work photography first, rating next to the call button, makes-serviced strip, phone and hours always visible.

## 11. What Andrew asked for, in his words (preferences to carry forward)

- Sites must match the look of the actual business; look for a real logo before making one.
- Use real reviews, location, services, and visuals.
- Images should be photorealistic, professional, "not too cartoonish" and "not bland".
- Use only free images that are legal to use.
- Study how other businesses build their sites and take inspiration.
- Process he wants: design decisions saved to DESIGN.md, three creative directions with one recommended, screenshots at 1440px and 390px, critique from screenshots, no generic AI look (no purple gradients, glass effects, repeated rounded cards).
- All finished sites go in the shared GitHub repo.
